# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate professionally and respectfully. Constructive technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- focusing criticism on the work rather than the person;
- explaining technical claims with evidence;
- respecting different experience levels and languages;
- accepting correction and revising inaccurate information;
- protecting confidential, personal, and customer information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not permitted:

- abusive, discriminatory, or sexually explicit content;
- repeated unwanted contact or intimidation;
- knowingly false security, accuracy, or product claims;
- attempts to obtain or distribute license bypasses, credentials, or private product code;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- spam, deceptive promotion, or unrelated solicitation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its users.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Do not use public issues when a report contains personal or sensitive information.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official eeFunction community spaces.
