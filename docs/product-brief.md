# eeFunction Product Brief

## Product definition

eeFunction is an Excel engineering calculation platform that provides traceable property functions and reusable calculation examples directly inside workbooks.

한국어 정의:

> eeFunction은 Excel 안에서 검증 가능한 물성 함수와 재사용 가능한 엔지니어링 계산 예제를 제공하는 전문 계산 플랫폼이다.

## Product editions

The exact commercial packaging is not final. The website must not present planned capabilities as released functionality.

### Free

Intended role:

- entry-level property functions;
- product evaluation and education;
- basic examples and documentation;
- community adoption.

### Pro

Intended role:

- advanced and industry-specific functions;
- official verified Excel examples;
- broader validation and compatibility information;
- commercial project usage under a Pro license;
- priority updates and support options.

### Team and Enterprise

Potential future roles:

- centralized license administration;
- organizational deployment;
- controlled update channels;
- procurement and compliance documentation;
- commercial support commitments.

No Team or Enterprise capability should be advertised until product requirements and delivery mechanisms are confirmed.

## Primary users

- process and mechanical engineers;
- energy and utility engineers;
- HVAC engineers;
- chemical and plant engineers;
- engineering consultants;
- technical educators and researchers;
- Excel-based calculation authors and reviewers.

## Core value propositions

1. Work directly in Excel without rebuilding common property calculations.
2. Reduce formula duplication and transcription errors.
3. Reuse official and community calculation examples.
4. Review applicable ranges, references, compatibility, and limitations.
5. Keep the workbook as the working engineering document.

## Trust requirements

For each technical function or example, the product should eventually disclose:

- model or reference source;
- applicable range;
- units and input conventions;
- validation method and tolerance;
- supported Excel environments;
- minimum eeFunction version;
- known limitations;
- last verification date.

## Product boundaries

The website is not a certification that a workbook is safe or fit for a particular design. Users remain responsible for engineering judgment, input verification, code compliance, and independent review where required.

The public website repository does not contain the proprietary calculation engine or commercial license implementation.

## Initial launch content

A credible first Pro launch should include at minimum:

- a clear Free versus Pro comparison;
- ten high-quality official examples;
- a searchable function catalogue;
- compatibility and data-handling pages;
- release notes and known limitations;
- pricing or a clear procurement path;
- installation, trial, and support instructions.
